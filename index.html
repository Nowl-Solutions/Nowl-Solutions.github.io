      In operation
      
Most of what Nowl catches never becomes an incident.

    Nobody gets woken twice
    If a signal recovers inside the grace window, Nowl closes it and files it in the morning digest instead of paging.
    
        
    Every flag shows its reasoning
    You see which features moved the score and by how much. Mark a bad call and the correction enters the next fit.
    
        
    Correlation before notification
    Related spikes across services collapse into one incident with a causal ordering, not forty separate notifications.
    
        
    Audit trail by default
    Every score, threshold change and acknowledgement is written to an immutable log with configurable retention.
